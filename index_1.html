Lessons in Pencil Publisher

Lessons in Pencil is a YouTube channel of short hand-drawn lessons about self-improvement, productivity and personal finance. Every video teaches one idea and one thing to try today.

Lessons in Pencil Publisher is the private tool the channel owner uses to publish these videos. Through the YouTube Data API it uploads videos to the Lessons in Pencil channel and manages their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and the channel's own description and banner.

Only the owner of the Lessons in Pencil channel uses this tool. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else and does not access any other YouTube channel.
